The label

Marcellis Music is an independent label. It is home to five artists: Tash Marcellis, Mike Ellis, Jordan Marceau, Rafa Marceau and Julia Ellis.

The sound runs wide. Afrosoul and R&B with Portuguese and Spanish code switch. Country, Brazilian ballads and forró. Hip hop and trap in Spanglish. Acoustic pop ballads. Outlaw country from Houston.

Every artist has their own YouTube channel, and the label channel brings them together. The music is out on all platforms through HyperFollow.
